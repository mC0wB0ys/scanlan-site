Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Overview

ScanLan is designed with a privacy-first model. Core network scanning runs on-device.

Data Collection

We do not collect analytics, usage tracking, or advertising identifiers from the app.

Network Data

When you scan a network, discovered device and service information is processed locally on your device for results, history, and export functionality.

Optional Context Lookups

Some features contact external services. Public-IP lookup contacts Amazon's checkip service, geolocation lookup sends the queried IP address to ipapi.co, and DNS, WHOIS and certificate inspection contact the relevant resolvers, servers or target hosts. Those services receive your connection's public IP address and the information needed for the request.

On-Device Insights and Shortcuts

When enabled and available, Apple Foundation Models generates Insights on your device. ScanLan does not send scan reports to an external AI provider or Private Cloud Compute. When AI is unavailable, local rules produce the report. Generated reports may be kept briefly in app memory to avoid repeating work; they are not added to a cloud account. Siri and Shortcuts can access scan names and counts to let you select and open saved reports.

Internet Speed Tests

Quick Network Check is available by default, and saved off preferences are respected. Tests run when you select Run Check or Retest on the dashboard, or when you enable automatic tests in Settings. Automatic tests are off by default. Tests transfer download/upload data to M-Lab, with Cloudflare as a fallback. M-Lab collects and publishes measurements including your public IP address. Provider retention and use are governed by their policies: M-Lab and Cloudflare. No scan report is included in speed-test payloads. You can disable Quick Network Check in Settings.

Your Choices

You can disable on-device AI or Quick Network Check in Settings. Delete saved scans in History to remove those records and clear the temporary Insights cache. Exported files remain wherever you chose to save or share them.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Exported files are generated at your request and shared only by your actions.

Permissions

ScanLan may request Local Network and location-related permissions to improve network visibility features such as SSID context.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Support page.
